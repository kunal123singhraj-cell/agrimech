Sentinel-2 Crop Residue Availability Estimation Architecture

AgriMech's remote sensing pipeline integrates multispectral satellite telemetry, agronomic crop phenology indices, and localized mass-balance ratios to predict agricultural residue generation with commercial precision.

01

Multispectral Imagery Acquisition

Surface reflectance bands from ESA Copernicus Sentinel-2 (B4 Red, B8 NIR, B8A Narrow NIR) are processed at 10m & 20m spatial resolution. Bottom-of-Atmosphere (Level-2A BOA) corrections isolate true vegetative spectra from atmospheric haze.

02

Crop Canopy & Yield Estimation

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) and Red Edge (NDRE) time-series track vegetative vigor, heading, and senescence. Supervised ground-truth classification maps crop type and calibrated yield per hectare.

03

Residue-to-Product Ratio (RPR)

Residue generation is modeled using ICAR / PAU agronomic ratios. For example, Paddy Straw has an RPR of 1.30, meaning 1.3 tonnes of stubble is produced for every 1 tonne of grain harvested.

04

Collection Availability Factor (CAF)

Crucially, not all residue can be gathered. The CAF parameter discounts livestock fodder consumption (up to 50% for Ragi), soil conservation blanketing, and local domestic uses, isolating only true net collectible biomass.
